# zingerBurger-novel-to-visuals-maker-using-flow-
How To Use -- 
Open Flow: Click "Open Flow" to sign in and open your Google Flow project. The dot must be green.
Input: Type a Title and paste your Chapter Text, OR upload .txt files for batch processing.
Settings: Select your preferred aspect ratio and generation mode (Parallel is faster).
Generate: Click ▶ Generate. Gemini will plan the storyboard, and Flow will generate the images automatically.
Download: Hover over any finished panel to download it individually. When the chapter finishes, a full ZIP and PDF will download automatically.
